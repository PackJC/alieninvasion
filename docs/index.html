Mission briefing

Something came down in the night.

A UFO crash event that works exactly like DayZ's vanilla helicopter crashes: the wreck is even built on the same CrashBase class. The difference: the site is wrapped in a toxic cloud, alien technology lies in the wreckage, and when survivors close in, 10–15 hostile aliens crawl out to meet them. Vanilla heli crashes are untouched and keep running alongside it.

UFO crash site

A custom wreck with green light, fire, a smoke column, crackling electrical sparks, mist, distant crash audio, and a toxic gas cloud that demands NBC gear.

Hostile visitors

Little Green Men with their own voices and footsteps. Up close they claw like infected; in the open they hit you with a psychic zap from up to 80 m.

Alien technology

Salvage the Montauk Rifle, plasma cartridges and plasma grenades from the wreck. Cells come in green, blue, lime and violet, and every colour hits aliens twice as hard as earthly bullets.

Rechargeable cells

Cartridges can't be unloaded: combine one with a 9V battery to recharge it. A full battery fills a full cartridge, and a cell cleared from the rifle's chamber loads back in.

Tin foil hat

It works. Aliens lose track of anyone wearing an intact foil hat unless they're within arm's reach, and their psychic zaps can't get through.

Alien harvest

Skin an alien for Area 51 Steaks and a Roswell Hide. Cooked steak gives 90 seconds of green night vision; the hide tans into green leather for backpacks and bags.

Server-tunable

Every number, from alien counts and loot odds to the gas zone, zap range and damage bonuses, lives in $profile:Gebs/alieninvasion.json.

Economy files generated

The server writes ready-made types, events, crash positions and loot points into $profile:Gebs/mpmissions/ on first start.

No dependency chain

Built to stand alone.

The mod declares no third-party runtime requirement. Load it on the server and every connecting client.

Contact sequence

How a crash plays out.

Think of it as a helicopter crash from another world: the Central Economy places the wreck exactly like a vanilla heli crash event, and the wreck then runs the encounter: effects, audio, toxic gas, salvage and its own alien spawns.

  1. 01

    Impact

    geb_Aliencrash enters the world through the Central Economy and plays the distant crash sound for everyone in range.

  2. 02

    Visual signal

    Clients attach green illumination, fire, a smoke column, sparks and ground mist to the crash object.

  3. 03

    Toxic cloud

    An 8 m gas zone settles over the wreck. It uses DayZ's own contaminated-area rules, so NBC gear protects you. It disappears with the wreck.

  4. 04

    Salvage

    A 35% chance of a Montauk Rifle with a cartridge, up to two partly charged spare cartridges and up to two plasma grenades lie 3–8 m from the wreck.

  5. 05

    Contact

    When a survivor comes within 300 m, 10–15 Little Green Men appear 5–25 m from the wreck. Until then nothing runs, so idle crash sites cost the server nothing.

  6. 06

    Psychic attack

    An alien chasing you with a clear line of sight zaps you from 4–80 m, at most once a minute each: a green beam from its head and a crackle where it hits. Up close they claw like infected. A foil hat blocks the zaps.

  7. 07

    Recovery

    Skin dead aliens for Area 51 Steak and Roswell Hide. When the event ends and no one is within 1 km, the wreck, its gas cloud and any surviving aliens are cleaned up together.

Montauk Rifle

A semi-automatic experimental rifle that fires plasma bolts in the loaded cell's colour. Plasma deals double damage to aliens; every other firearm deals 75%. Skeleton stock with a cheek rest, a shaped pistol grip, hooded iron sights with a glowing dot, a live read-out on the back, purple fluid swirling in the vent tubes, lime fluid flowing behind the glass windows, and electric arcs on the muzzle when it runs hot.

Deployment protocol

Install Alien Invasion.

Choose the player path for local use or the server path for a community server. The Workshop remains the authorized distribution source.

  1. 1

    Open the Workshop page

    Visit Alien Invasion on Steam and select Subscribe.

  2. 2

    Let Steam download the mod

    Wait for the DayZ Workshop download to finish before starting the launcher.

  3. 3

    Join a compatible server

    The official DayZ launcher can resolve and load the server's required Workshop mods automatically.

Open Workshop item

  1. 1

    Download the Workshop item

    Subscribe with the server's Steam account or retrieve Workshop item 2840597308 using your normal SteamCMD workflow.

  2. 2

    Install and key the server

    Place the published mod folder beside the DayZ server executable. Copy its .bikey file into the server's keys directory.

  3. 3

    Add the launch parameter

    Load the mod in your server startup command. Keep the actual installed folder name if it differs from the example.

    -mod=@alieninvasion
  4. 4

    Start the server once

    On startup the mod writes its config and Central Economy files into the server profile folder (the one your -profiles= launch parameter points to), next to Gebsfish's files if you run it. They are only rewritten when the mod version changes, so your edits survive restarts.

    <profile>/Gebs/
├── alieninvasion.json                  ← server settings (see Config)
└── mpmissions/
    ├── alieninvasion-types.xml
    ├── alieninvasion-spawnabletypes.xml
    ├── alieninvasion-events.xml
    ├── alieninvasion-cfgeconomycore.xml
    ├── alieninvasion-cfgeventspawns.xml
    └── alieninvasion-mapgroupproto.xml
  5. 5

    Register the economy files

    Copy the types, spawnabletypes and events files into a new alieninvasion folder inside your mission (mpmissions/dayzOffline.<map>/alieninvasion/), then paste the block from alieninvasion-cfgeconomycore.xml inside <economycore> in the mission's cfgeconomycore.xml. Your own types.xml and events.xml stay untouched.

  6. 6

    Merge positions and loot points

    Paste the StaticAlienCrash event from alieninvasion-cfgeventspawns.xml into cfgeventspawns.xml, and the geb_Aliencrash group from alieninvasion-mapgroupproto.xml into mapgroupproto.xml. On Chernarus you get 79 hand-picked crash sites; on any other map the generator borrows that mission's heli-crash positions.

  7. 7

    Restart and verify

    Restart, check the script and RPT logs for [AlienInvasion] lines, then visit a crash: loot and gas are there immediately, aliens appear once you're within 300 m.

Central Economy reference

What the generated files contain.

If you have ever configured DayZ's StaticHeliCrash event, this is the identical pattern. The wreck is registered in types.xml, the event is defined in events.xml, and its positions live in cfgeventspawns.xml, exactly like a heli crash. The only difference is what happens on site: the UFO script handles the aliens, loot and gas itself. You never add alien spawns, infected events or territories for it.

Prefer to paste by hand, or with a hosting panel's economy editor? These are the same entries the generator writes. Stop the server and back up the mission folder before editing it.

Important update

Do not copy the old Steam tutorial's InfectedAlien event or its <secondary>InfectedAlien</secondary> line. The wreck script already creates 10–15 aliens around each UFO. Keeping the old secondary event can produce duplicate or excessive alien spawns.

  1. 1

    Register the wreck in db/types.xml

    Paste this entry anywhere inside the file's existing <types> ... </types> element. Do not add a second root element. The generated file also registers every item, the meat and the hide.

    db/types.xml
    <type name="geb_Aliencrash">
    <nominal>0</nominal>
    <lifetime>2100</lifetime>
    <restock>0</restock>
    <min>0</min>
    <quantmin>-1</quantmin>
    <quantmax>-1</quantmax>
    <cost>100</cost>
    <flags count_in_cargo="0" count_in_hoarder="0" count_in_map="1" count_in_player="0" crafted="0" deloot="0"/>
</type>
  2. 2

    Define the event in db/events.xml

    Paste this inside the existing <events> ... </events> element. nominal is the target number of active crashes; lower it if three encounters are too many for your server.

    db/events.xml
    <event name="StaticAlienCrash">
    <nominal>3</nominal>
    <min>0</min>
    <max>0</max>
    <lifetime>2100</lifetime>
    <restock>0</restock>
    <saferadius>1000</saferadius>
    <distanceradius>1000</distanceradius>
    <cleanupradius>1000</cleanupradius>
    <flags deletable="1" init_random="0" remove_damaged="0"/>
    <position>fixed</position>
    <limit>child</limit>
    <active>1</active>
    <children>
        <child lootmax="15" lootmin="10" max="3" min="1" type="geb_Aliencrash"/>
    </children>
</event>
  3. 3

    Add locations to cfgeventspawns.xml

    Paste the event inside <eventposdef>. The generated file already contains real positions; if you write it by hand, replace every example coordinate with a safe, open location from your map. Add as many <pos> lines as you want; x and z are map coordinates and a is rotation.

    cfgeventspawns.xml
    <event name="StaticAlienCrash">
    <zone smin="1" smax="3" dmin="3" dmax="5" r="45"/>
    <!-- Replace these examples with coordinates for your map. -->
    <pos x="7500.0" z="7500.0" a="0.0"/>
    <pos x="8000.0" z="7200.0" a="90.0"/>
</event>
  4. 4

    Give the wreck loot points in mapgroupproto.xml

    The event asks for 10–15 loot items at each wreck, and the Central Economy can only place them where the wreck's group defines points. Paste the generated geb_Aliencrash group inside <prototype>. It uses vanilla Military usage (heli-crash loot); its comment explains how to switch to a custom UFO usage instead.

  5. 5

    Validate, restart and test

    Check that each classname and event name matches exactly, validate the XML files, and restart the server. Watch the server RPT and script logs for XML or CE errors. To test quickly, spawn geb_Aliencrash with an admin tool on a dedicated server (it runs the full encounter too), or temporarily set nominal to 1.

What each file does: types.xml registers the spawnable objects, events.xml controls the dynamic event, cfgeventspawns.xml supplies its possible positions, and mapgroupproto.xml gives the wreck its loot points. Based on the original Alien Invasion tutorial, updated for the current scripted encounter. See Bohemia's Central Economy reference for the underlying file format.

Safe to edit

Your values are kept.

A broken file is never overwritten: the server logs the error and uses defaults for that session. The file is only rewritten on a mod update, which adds any new settings without touching yours. Out-of-range values are clamped (minimum never above maximum, chances 0–1).

Troubleshooting

Questions.

Does the mod replace vanilla helicopter crashes?

No. Alien Invasion adds its own separate event that behaves like a heli crash (same dynamic-event system, same setup files) but with its own UFO wreck class and sound set. Vanilla crash events keep running untouched.

Where do the aliens come from? Do I configure their spawns?

The wreck spawns them itself. Once a player comes within 300 m (CrashAliensSpawnDistance), the wreck places 10–15 aliens at random positions 5–25 m around it, and removes any survivors when it despawns. Do not add infected events, zombie territories or any alien spawn configuration: that would only create duplicates.

Why did I spawn the static wreck and get no aliens?

StaticObj_geb_Aliencrash is visual scenery only. Use geb_Aliencrash, through the Central Economy or with an admin tool on a dedicated server, for the scripted encounter.

Something keeps hitting me from far away. What is it?

A psychic zap: an alien chasing you, with a clear line of sight, hits you from 4–80 m, at most once a minute each: a green beam from the alien's head and a crackle where it lands. Break line of sight, close the distance, or wear a tin foil hat, which blocks the zaps completely. Server owners can tune or disable it in alieninvasion.json.

Is the gas around the wreck dangerous?

Yes. It's DayZ's own contaminated-area effect, so a gas mask and NBC suit protect you. The salvage lies inside it. Server owners can turn it off with CrashGasZone.

How do I recharge plasma cartridges?

Combine a cartridge with a charged 9V battery (drag one onto the other, or hold one and use the action on the other). A full battery refills a whole cartridge; a partial one adds as many cells as its charge covers.

Does every player need the mod?

Yes. It contains custom models, textures, sounds, particles, scripts and localization, so it must run on the server and all clients.

Does Alien Invasion require Community Framework?

No third-party mod is declared as a runtime dependency in the source.

Where are the mission XML files?

The server writes them on first start into $profile:Gebs/mpmissions/ (see Setup). The mod never edits your mission folder itself: you copy the files in, so your own balance stays yours.

I changed alieninvasion.json and nothing happened.

The file is read when the server starts, so restart after editing. If the server log shows [AlienInvasion] alieninvasion.json failed to load, the JSON has a syntax error (often a missing comma); the server runs on defaults until it's fixed, and your file is left untouched.

Can I include this in a server pack?

No. Subscribe to the authorized Workshop release and use a Steam Collection for your server's mod list.

What's new.

What changed in each release. Server owners: 2.0.0 rewrites the generated economy files. Copy the new types file into your mission again and replace the geb_Aliencrash group in mapgroupproto.xml.

2.0

Version 2.0.0: new

  • Plasma Grenade (geb_PlasmaGrenade): an alien sticky grenade that clings to whatever it hits and bursts in a blue plasma blast 3 seconds after leaving the hand. Found at crash sites and sometimes on aliens.
  • Specimen Tank (geb_AlienTank): a sleeping alien in a tank of glowing green fluid, with bubbles, motes, a green glow and a hum. A prop for admins and map makers.
  • Four plasma colours: Montauk Cartridges come in green, blue, lime and violet, each with its own muzzle effect, plasma ball, light and a glowing window on the cartridge.
  • Montauk Rifle rebuilt: a skeleton stock, a shaped pistol grip and trigger guard, hooded iron sights, an animated read-out on the back, purple fluid in the vent tubes, lime fluid behind the glass windows, new detailed textures, and blue-white arcs on the muzzle when it runs hot.
  • The aliens' psychic zap is a green beam from the alien's head to you; a dying alien bursts green instead of firing a laser.
  • Crash sites smoke like a heli crash and crackle with electrical sparks; aliens bleed green and have a fuller voice of 75 takes.
  • Plasma Cell (Ammo_Plasma_Cell): clearing a loaded rifle's chamber gives the cell back, and it loads into any cartridge. It never spawns as loot.
2.0

Version 2.0.0: fixes

  • Aliens can be skinned. They inherited vanilla's infected rule that bodies can't be, so there were no steaks or hides. A frozen alien can't be skinned, and anything an alien carries drops beside it.
  • Aliens have their hit zones back: headshots do extra damage and can kill outright. The Little Green Man's config threw the zones away when it changed its health.
  • The Roswell Hide's condition follows the alien's torso (it read two animal zones the alien doesn't have), and it is wolf-pelt sized: 6 kg, 5×3, up to 4 leather by hand or 6 in a barrel instead of a bear's 15 kg and 12.
  • Crash loot lies on the crater around the UFO. The loot points were copied from vanilla's Mi-8 wreck, mostly outside the UFO and underground.
  • The 10% spare cartridge aliens can carry now spawns.
  • Server crash fixed when a loaded Montauk Rifle showed a chambered round (admin spawns with ammo, restarts with a loaded rifle in storage).
  • Montauk Rifle: no more invisible Ruger scope, suppressor and wrap; weighs 3.4 kg instead of 350 g, lies properly on the ground, and its shots carry 2 km like other rifles'.
  • Plasma shots show their cell's colour: the laser stayed green for every cell. The cloud of glowing dots that sprayed from a hot rifle is gone.
  • Aliens, the crash site and the rifle cast shadows; models step down in detail with distance; the hull, rifle and cartridge show their surface detail.
  • Aliens move, sound and get hit like infected (rebuilt rig, vanilla swing and footstep sounds, flesh hit sounds). The Tin Foil Hat lies on the ground properly.
  • Sound and ammo-type classes renamed with the geb_ prefix so other mods can't clash with them; new strings translated into all 13 languages.
  • Aliens’ resistance to firearms applies before a hit lands, so it also saves them from shots that would otherwise kill. A multiplier of zero makes them immune to firearms, and the plasma multipliers keep the hit zone and the impact speed.
1.2

Version 1.2.0: Roswell Hide crafting

  • The Roswell Hide tans into green Roswell Leather with garden lime, by hand or in a barrel. (It used to give ordinary brown leather.)
  • Roswell Leather sews into a green Roswell Leather Backpack; the hide and a rope make a Roswell Hide Courier Bag, which takes three sticks to become a Roswell Hide Backpack. All of them break back down like vanilla's.
  • Reworked textures: the hide looks like the alien's skin, the steak is green at every cooking stage (it turned back into beef when cooked), the Montauk Rifle is twice the resolution, and the tin foil hat reflects silver instead of gold.
  • Four new items in the generated types file; new strings translated into all 13 languages.

  • Aliens have voices and footsteps. They were silent and filled server logs with "No sound callback" warnings.
  • Alien attacks no longer land invisible hits from 100 m every 100 seconds: up close they use normal infected melee, at range the new visible psychic zap.
  • Surviving aliens are removed with their wreck instead of piling up for the whole server uptime.
  • Vanilla helicopter and sleigh crashes keep their distant crash sound with the mod loaded.
  • The wreck's green light and fire/mist effects are removed when it despawns.
  • Aliens spawn on the ground, in the configured 10–15 count.
  • Skinning an alien now gives the Roswell Hide (the config named a class that didn't exist).
  • The tin foil hat shows its name instead of its description, is hat-sized (3×2, 60 g), and no longer loads missing damage materials.
  • The UFO wreck no longer needs another mod's Mi-8 textures.
  • Plasma shots play one muzzle effect instead of two and stop logging "Plasma_Shot" warnings.
  • Config patches no longer reuse a vanilla patch name and load after every vanilla class they extend.
  • Removed the alien preparation recipe, which could never run (recipes can't use a corpse); skinning gives the steaks.
